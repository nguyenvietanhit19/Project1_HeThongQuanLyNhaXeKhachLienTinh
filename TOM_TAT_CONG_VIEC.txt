sinh ra de san bot viec quan ly nhan su van hanh khoi quan_ly khi quy mo cong

nhan su cap van hanh - khong co quyen nghiep vu nao khac (khong sua tuyen,

--- UC-47. Quan ly ho so nhan su van hanh (tai xe/phu xe) ---
Actor: Quan ly nhan su, hoac Quan ly.
Tien dieu kien: Voi ho so phu xe: tai khoan phu_xe da duoc tao (UC-36). Tai
xe khong can tai khoan (muc 3.2).
Luong chinh (muc 8.9 diem 5, muc 3.2):
  1. Chon "Them ho so", hoac chon 1 ho so co san de sua.
  2. Nhap ho ten, so dien thoai, chuc danh (tai_xe/phu_xe), so CCCD, ngay
     sinh, ngay vao lam; neu la phu xe thi chon tai khoan phu_xe tuong ung.
  3. Nhap/cap nhat giay to: bang lai va giay kham suc khoe - moi giay to
     gom so, hang (chi bang lai), ngay cap, ngay het han.
  4. He thong kiem tra, luu ho so va ghi nhat ky thao tac (UC-50).
Luong re nhanh:
  - Tai buoc 2: chuc danh phu_xe nhung tai khoan duoc chon khong phai vai
    tro phu_xe, hoac da gan voi ho so khac -> chan, bao loi -> chon lai.
  - Tai buoc 2: chuc danh tai_xe ma co chon tai khoan -> chan (tai xe khong
    co tai khoan, muc 3.2).
  - Tai buoc 3: ho so tai_xe chua co bang lai -> chan, bao "tai xe bat
    buoc co thong tin bang lai".
  - Tai buoc 3: ngay het han da qua -> van cho luu (cap nhat ho so cu), he
    thong hien thi co "da het han" - UC-48 se nhac ngay o lan quet ke tiep.
  - Tai buoc 3: gia han giay to (nhap ngay het han moi) -> he thong xoa co
    da canh bao, chu ky nhac bat dau lai tu dau.
  - Khong co thao tac xoa ho so, khong doi chuc danh sau khi tao; nhan su
    thoi viec dung UC-49.
Hau dieu kien: Ho so day du; nhan su dang lam viec co the duoc quan_ly dua
vao bien che xe (UC-35).

--- UC-48. Tu dong canh bao giay to nhan su van hanh sap het han/da het han (job) ---
Actor: He thong (job dinh ky - khong phai actor nguoi dung thao tac truc tiep).
Tien dieu kien: Ho so nhan su dang_lam co giay to voi ngay het han con <= 30
ngay (nguong do quan_ly cau hinh, muc 8.7 diem 3) hoac da qua.
Luong chinh (muc 8.9 diem 5):
  1. Job quet giay to cua toan bo nhan su dang lam viec, moi ngay 1 lan.
  2. Giay to con <= 30 ngay ma chua co co "da canh bao sap het han" -> bat
     co, gui thong bao toi moi tai khoan quan_ly_nhan_su dang hoat dong
     (neu ho ten, loai giay to, ngay het han).
  3. Giay to da qua ngay het han ma chua co co "da canh bao het han" -> bat
     co, gui thong bao muc khan toi cung nhom nguoi nhan.
Luong re nhanh:
  - Khong co tai khoan quan_ly_nhan_su nao dang hoat dong -> gui cho quan_ly.
  - Nhan su da da_nghi_viec -> bo qua.
  - Giay to duoc gia han (UC-47) -> 2 co duoc xoa, giay to quay lai chu ky

--- UC-49. Cho nghi viec nhan su van hanh ---
Actor: Quan ly nhan su, hoac Quan ly.
Tien dieu kien: Ho so dang dang_lam. Chi ap dung cho nhan su van hanh (tai

Luong chinh (muc 8.9 diem 6, muc 3.2):

  2. He thong liet ke cac dong bien che xe_nhan_su ma nguoi nay dang thuoc,

     chuyen cho ho ngay.
  5. He thong gui thong bao toi quan_ly (de go han/bien che nguoi moi,
     UC-35) va toi cac dieu do vien (de dung nguon du phong tam thoi, muc

  6. Ghi nhat ky thao tac (UC-50).
Luong re nhanh:
  - Tai buoc 1: xe ma nguoi nay thuoc bien che dang co chuyen dang_chay ->
    chan, bao "cho chuyen ket thuc roi thuc hien lai" -> ket thuc.
  - Tai buoc 2: nguoi nay khong thuoc bien che xe nao -> bo qua buoc 2 va

khong dang nhap duoc; cac xe bi anh huong da duoc bao cho quan_ly va dieu

"""Upload/xoa anh tren Cloudinary - phuc vu anh ho so nhan su van hanh
(UC-47, NGHIEP_VU.md muc 8.9) va anh giay to (bang lai, giay kham suc
khoe - UC-47/48). Xem quyet dinh dung lai Cloudinary o
NHAP_QUAN_LY_NHAN_SU.md muc 3.0 va cau truc bang o database_quanLyNhanSu.md
muc 1/2.

Service ha tang (goi dich vu ngoai) - khong thuoc Repository, dat o
services/ theo dung ARCHITECTURE.md muc 2 (cung nhom voi email_service.py).
File nay CHI lo viec ky thuat (tai len/sinh URL xem/xoa) - khong tu quyet
dinh khi nao duoc phep upload, anh cua ai, hay ghi nhat ky: nhung viec do
thuoc ve Service nghiep vu goi toi day (VD ho_so_nhan_su_service.py, chua
viet), dung nguyen tac tach lop cua du an.

Quan trong - luu `public_id`, KHONG luu URL co dinh vao DB: anh tai len
bang delivery kieu `authenticated` (Cloudinary bat buoc URL co chu ky moi
xem duoc, khong phai URL public ai doan cung ra) - hop voi du lieu nhay
cam nhu CCCD/giay to. Vi vay:
  - `nhan_su_van_hanh.anh_ho_so` / `giay_to_nhan_su.anh_giay_to` nen luu
    `public_id` ma tai_anh_len() tra ve, KHONG luu URL.
  - Moi lan can hien thi anh (VD tra ve cho frontend), goi lay_url_xem()
    de lay URL co chu ky - khong cache/luu lai URL nay.
"""

import uuid

import cloudinary
import cloudinary.uploader
import cloudinary.utils

from app.config import CLOUDINARY_URL

cloudinary.config(cloudinary_url=CLOUDINARY_URL)

# Thu muc tren Cloudinary - tach rieng anh chan dung va anh giay to cho de
# quan ly, khong lien quan toi cau truc bang trong DB.
THU_MUC_ANH_HO_SO = "nha_xe/nhan_su/ho_so"
THU_MUC_ANH_GIAY_TO = "nha_xe/nhan_su/giay_to"


def tai_anh_len(du_lieu_anh: bytes, thu_muc: str) -> str | None:
    """Upload 1 anh len Cloudinary, tra ve `public_id` de Service nghiep vu
    luu vao DB (KHONG tra URL - xem docstring dau file).

    `du_lieu_anh`: noi dung file anh dang bytes (doc tu UploadFile cua
    FastAPI, VD `await file.read()`).
    `thu_muc`: 1 trong 2 hang so THU_MUC_ANH_* o tren.

    Tra `None` neu upload that bai - Service goi ham nay tu quyet dinh co
    raise LoiHeThong hay khong (giong cach mat_khau_service.py xu ly ket
    qua bool cua gui_mail() o email_service.py), file nay khong tu raise
    loi nghiep vu.
    """
    try:
        ket_qua = cloudinary.uploader.upload(
            du_lieu_anh,
            folder=thu_muc,
            public_id=str(uuid.uuid4()),  # tu sinh - khong dung ten file goc
            type="authenticated",  # bat buoc co chu ky moi xem duoc
            resource_type="image",
        )
        return ket_qua["public_id"]
    except Exception as loi:
        print(f"Loi upload anh len Cloudinary: {loi}")
        return None


def lay_url_xem(public_id: str) -> str:
    """Sinh URL co chu ky de xem 1 anh da upload - Cloudinary chi tra anh
    khi chu ky dung, chan duoc viec doan mo URL ngau nhien.

    Luu y: chu ky nay KHONG tu het han theo thoi gian (khac co che OTP/JWT
    dang dung trong du an). Muon co URL tu het han that can bat them tinh
    nang "Token-based authentication" rieng cua Cloudinary (cau hinh them
    o Dashboard, chua kiem tra co nam trong free tier hay khong) - CHUA
    lam o ban nay.
    """
    url, _ = cloudinary.utils.cloudinary_url(
        public_id,
        type="authenticated",
        resource_type="image",
        sign_url=True,
    )
    return url


def xoa_anh(public_id: str) -> bool:
    """Xoa 1 anh khoi Cloudinary theo `public_id` - goi khi nhan su thay
    anh moi (VD gia han giay to, anh cu khong con can giu) de tranh ton
    rac tren free tier.

    Tra `False` neu xoa that bai - Service goi tu quyet dinh co coi la
    loi nghiem trong hay bo qua (anh rac khong anh huong van hanh ngay).
    """
    try:
        ket_qua = cloudinary.uploader.destroy(public_id, type="authenticated", resource_type="image")
        return ket_qua.get("result") == "ok"
    except Exception as loi:
        print(f"Loi xoa anh tren Cloudinary: {loi}")
        return False

-- Dia diem & tuyen (DATABASE.md muc 2)

CREATE TABLE khu_vuc (
    id            UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    ten           TEXT NOT NULL,
    tinh_thanh    TEXT NOT NULL
);

CREATE TABLE diem_don_tra (
    id            UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    khu_vuc_id    UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES khu_vuc(id),
    ten           TEXT NOT NULL,
    dia_chi       TEXT NOT NULL,
    loai          TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (loai IN ('van_phong', 'diem_dung'))
);

CREATE TABLE nhom_tuyen (
    id            UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    ten           TEXT NOT NULL
);

CREATE TABLE tuyen (
    id              UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    nhom_tuyen_id   UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES nhom_tuyen(id),
    ten             TEXT NOT NULL
);

CREATE TABLE tuyen_diem_don_tra (
    id                        UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    tuyen_id                  UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES tuyen(id),
    diem_don_tra_id           UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES diem_don_tra(id),
    thu_tu                    INTEGER NOT NULL,
    thoi_gian_du_kien_phut    INTEGER NOT NULL,

    UNIQUE (tuyen_id, thu_tu),
    UNIQUE (tuyen_id, diem_don_tra_id)
);

-- Xe & bien che (DATABASE.md muc 1.5, 3.1, 3.2)

CREATE TABLE loai_xe (
    id            UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    ten           TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE,
    he_so_gia     NUMERIC(4,2) NOT NULL DEFAULT 1.0,
    so_do_ghe     JSONB NOT NULL
);

CREATE TABLE xe (
    id              UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    bien_so         TEXT NOT NULL UNIQUE,
    loai_xe_id      UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES loai_xe(id),
    trang_thai      TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'hoat_dong' CHECK (trang_thai IN ('hoat_dong', 'bao_tri', 'ngung_su_dung')),
    diem_goc_id     UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES diem_don_tra(id),
    nhom_tuyen_id   UUID NULL REFERENCES nhom_tuyen(id)
);

CREATE TABLE xe_nhan_su (
    id                      UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    xe_id                   UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES xe(id),
    nhan_su_van_hanh_id     UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES nhan_su_van_hanh(id),
    loai                    TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (loai IN ('co_dinh', 'tam_thoi')),
    trang_thai              TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'dang_hoat_dong' CHECK (trang_thai IN ('dang_hoat_dong', 'tam_nghi')),
    ngay_bat_dau            TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),
    ngay_ket_thuc           TIMESTAMPTZ NULL
);

-- Lich chay dinh ky (DATABASE.md muc 3.5)

CREATE TABLE lich_chay_dinh_ky (
    id              UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    tuyen_id        UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES tuyen(id),
    gio_khoi_hanh   TIME NOT NULL,
    loai_xe_id      UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES loai_xe(id),
    dang_ap_dung    BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT true,
    ngay_tao        TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now()
);

-- Bang that su cua dieu do vien (nguoi 4, CONTRIBUTING.md muc 2 -
-- chuyen_xe_repository/chuyen_xe_service). Phuc vu UC-19, 20, 39, 40, 44, 45
-- (NGHIEP_VU.md muc 8.6, 12). Xem database_dieuDoVien.md muc 1-3 cho chi

-- Phu thuoc khoa ngoai vao cac bang o migration lien truoc
-- (20260927_1100_tao_bang_danh_muc_xe_tuyen_tam_thoi.sql) - file do la
-- TAM THOI, file nay thi KHONG tam thoi.

CREATE TABLE chuyen_xe (
    id                              UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    tuyen_id                        UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES tuyen(id),
    xe_id                           UUID NULL REFERENCES xe(id),
    loai_xe_id                      UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES loai_xe(id),
    lich_chay_dinh_ky_id            UUID NULL REFERENCES lich_chay_dinh_ky(id),
    xe_thuc_te_id                   UUID NULL REFERENCES xe(id),
    gio_khoi_hanh                   TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL,

    trang_thai                      TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'chua_khoi_hanh'
                                        CHECK (trang_thai IN (
                                            'chua_khoi_hanh', 'dang_chay', 'gap_su_co',
                                            'hoan_thanh', 'da_huy'
                                        )),

    dang_hoan                       BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT false,
    gio_xac_nhan_xuat_phat          TIMESTAMPTZ NULL,
    gio_hoan_thanh                  TIMESTAMPTZ NULL,
    loai_su_co                      TEXT NULL CHECK (loai_su_co IN ('loi_nha_xe', 'loi_khach_quan')),
    ly_do_su_co                     TEXT NULL,
    co_canh_bao_xung_dot_vi_tri     BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT false,
    ngay_tao                        TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now()
);

CREATE INDEX chuyen_xe_theo_xe_va_gio
    ON chuyen_xe (xe_id, gio_khoi_hanh);

CREATE INDEX chuyen_xe_dang_chay_thay
    ON chuyen_xe (xe_id)
    WHERE xe_thuc_te_id IS NOT NULL;

CREATE INDEX chuyen_xe_chua_gan_xe
    ON chuyen_xe (xe_id)
    WHERE xe_id IS NULL;


CREATE TABLE lich_su_diem_dung_chuyen (
    id                  UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    chuyen_id           UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES chuyen_xe(id),
    diem_don_tra_id     UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES diem_don_tra(id),
    gio_thuc_te         TIMESTAMPTZ NOT NULL DEFAULT now(),

    UNIQUE (chuyen_id, diem_don_tra_id)
);
